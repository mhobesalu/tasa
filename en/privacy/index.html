Privacy policy

Version 1.10, November 2026.

TASA is a study planner for pupils, free for families. This policy explains in plain language what data TASA uses, where it is, who sees it, and the rights of the pupil and the parent.

In short, for children

TASA helps you see what to do today, in small steps. Everything you put into TASA (homework, tests, your plan, screenshots) stays on your phone. We can’t see it, and neither can your teachers. Your data is never sold and there are no ads. In basic school, your parent gives consent for you to use TASA. If you are 13 or older, you also say yourself that you agree. You can say at any time that you don’t want to any more: tell your parent or email maria@tasaplan.ee. Nobody will be upset. Your parent can delete everything with one button.

1. Who is responsible

The controller is Sild Growth OÜ (registry code 14067479), Telliskivi 33-1, 10611 Tallinn, Estonia. Questions and requests: maria@tasaplan.ee, +372 5826 2802.

TASA keeps the pupil’s data on the phone only. There is no server. We cannot see the data on the phone and it goes nowhere. Sild Growth OÜ decides how the app handles data and is responsible for the data we receive ourselves: pilot consent forms, the parent’s name and email, notes from the talks and questionnaire answers (see section 2).

School pilot: the school sends the information letter to families through its own contacts and does not give us a pupil list or any e-diary data. The school is the controller of its own data (pupil list, e-diary). The school is not our processor and we are not the school’s processor; the roles are agreed with the school in writing. If the school collects the signed consent forms, it hands them over to us and keeps no copy.

2. What data TASA uses

On the pupil’s phone (we cannot see it):

  • the pupil’s name (first and last name, or a nickname) and class;
  • homework, deadlines, tests and review questions that the parent or pupil adds from an e-diary screenshot, by copying, or by hand;
  • grades needed to plan a retake, and the part of a teacher’s message that the user chooses to add to the plan (for example an event time or a note on a homework task);
  • the screenshots the text was read from (they stay in the phone’s photo gallery);
  • the plan and its progress: which steps were done and when, the daily study time, the timetable;
  • the reward settings and balance the parent chose;
  • the parent PIN that protects the parent view.

How the day felt: at the end of the day the pupil may mark how the day felt (good, okay, hard or very hard). This stays only on their phone. The parent, the school and we cannot see it. TASA uses it only to offer a lighter day after several hard days. No conclusions about health are drawn from it.

At Sild Growth OÜ (school pilot only):

  • the consent form: the pupil’s name and class, the parent’s name and email, signatures, the boxes ticked and the date;
  • the email address linked to the phone’s Apple or Google account, so we can send the install link (TestFlight or Google Play testing);
  • notes from the start and end talks;
  • questionnaire answers, linked to a self-chosen code, not a name.

What is required and what is optional: no data is required by law. To take part in the pilot we need the consent form, and an email to install the app. Talks, questionnaires, screenshots and rewards are optional; tasks can also be added by hand.

What we do not collect: diagnoses or health data, e-diary passwords, location, contacts, advertising or tracking data. TASA never logs into the e-diary, shows no ads and never sells data.

3. Why we use data and on what legal basis

Purpose Legal basis
Building and showing the pupil’s daily plan on the phone, counting rewards Consent (GDPR art 6(1)(a), Estonian Personal Data Protection Act § 8)
Taking part in the pilot and installing the app (consent form, email) Consent to take part in the pilot
Evaluating the pilot: talks Separate consent (its own box on the consent form)
Evaluating the pilot: questionnaires Separate consent (its own box on the consent form)
Answering a school’s or partner’s enquiry sent through the website form Legitimate interest in answering the enquiry (art 6(1)(f))

Legitimate interest balancing: interest form data is used only to answer the enquiry, which the school or partner sent itself and expects an answer to. The impact on people is small and outweighed by our interest in answering. You can object at maria@tasaplan.ee.

Who gives consent: for a pupil under 13, a parent or guardian consents. A pupil aged 13 or older gives their own consent, because the law requires it. Our rule is stricter: for a basic-school pupil we always also ask for a parent’s consent. One parent’s consent is enough; if it turns out the other parent does not agree, there is no valid consent and we stop processing. We ask for separate consent for each purpose and keep a record of when and how consent was given.

Withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it. Email maria@tasaplan.ee, tell the pilot school’s contact person, or tell us at a talk. No reason is needed. You can delete the data on the phone yourself with “Kustuta kõik andmed” (Delete all data) in the parent view, or by removing the app. If a pupil withdraws consent, we tell the parent straight away. Withdrawal does not make earlier processing unlawful, but we stop processing and delete the data as described in section 8.

4. Reading screenshots and automated decisions

To read text from screenshots, the app uses Google’s ML Kit, which runs on the phone. The picture and the text never leave the phone. ML Kit sends Google anonymous technical information (phone model, app version, an install identifier, speed and errors) so Google can improve the tool. Homework, grades and pictures are not part of it. Before anything is added, the user sees the list and can correct it.

TASA uses no external AI service. TASA does not solve problems, create practice tasks or assess the pupil.

The plan follows simple rules (nearest deadline first, steps of about 25 minutes). The plan is a suggestion: the pupil and parent can change, postpone or excuse steps. We make no automated decisions about the pupil with legal or similarly significant effects.

5. Who sees the data

  • The pupil sees their daily plan and reward balance.
  • The parent sees the parent view on the same phone, protected by a PIN. The pupil cannot see the parent view.
  • Teachers and the school do not see the pupil’s plan or data. At the end of the pilot the school gets a summary in which no pupil can be identified; results for very small groups are not shown separately.
  • Sild Growth OÜ cannot see the data on the phone. Only the TASA team sees consent forms, talk notes and questionnaire answers. At the end talk we look together with the family, on their phone, at how many steps were done.
  • Service providers: an email service (our correspondence with families), and Apple and Google for distributing the test version of the app (they receive the email given for installing). No learning data about the pupil is shared with them.

6. The tasaplan.ee website

The website uses no cookies and stores nothing on your device. The demo runs only in your browser and sends us no data.

  • Interest form: when a school or partner fills in the form, we receive the school or organisation, the contact name and role, email, which e-diary they use and any message. The form is delivered to our email by Formspree (Formspree Inc., USA). We keep this data as long as the conversation needs it, and no longer than 2 years.
  • Visit statistics: we count page visits with GoatCounter (operated from Ireland, servers in Finland and Germany). It shows us only totals: how often pages were viewed, which site visitors came from, browser, device, language and country. GoatCounter uses no cookies, does not store IP addresses and does not identify visitors. To tell repeat visits apart, a combination of IP address and browser details is kept in memory only, for up to 8 hours. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in knowing whether the website is used (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Hosting and fonts: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA) and its fonts come from Google Fonts (Google, USA). For technical reasons these services see the visitor’s IP address.

Transfers to these US services rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses. No child or family data from the TASA app is sent to these services.

7. Where data is stored

The pupil’s data is only on the family’s phone. Sild Growth OÜ keeps pilot data (consent forms, email, notes, questionnaires) in Estonia and the European Union. Apple and Google (USA) receive the email given for installing; this transfer relies on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

TASA makes no automatic cloud backup. On Android, copying TASA’s data to the Google account backup is turned off. On an iPhone, the phone’s own iCloud backup may include TASA’s data if the family has turned it on; the family manages this in their own Apple account and Sild Growth OÜ cannot see these backups.

In the parent view, the parent can save a backup file themselves (for example for a new phone) and keep it wherever they like. The phone creates the file; TASA does not send it anywhere and Sild Growth OÜ cannot see it. The file does not contain the PIN or the student’s mood answers.

8. How long we keep data

  • Data on the phone: until the family deletes it (“Kustuta kõik andmed” or removing the app).
  • Email given for installing: removed from the testers list at the end of the pilot.
  • Parent’s name and email, talk notes, questionnaire answers: deleted no later than 3 months after the pilot ends. Only totals from the questionnaires, from which nobody can be identified, are kept.
  • After someone withdraws or asks for deletion, we delete the data we hold within 30 days.
  • Proof of consent (who agreed to what, and when): for as long as we process the data and up to 3 years after the pilot ends, so we can show consent if needed.

9. Your rights

For themselves and for their child, the parent can:

  • see what data we hold and get a copy;
  • have wrong data corrected;
  • delete all data on the phone with one button, and ask by email for the data we hold to be deleted;
  • save the data on the phone to a backup file (“Salvesta varukoopia” in the parent view);
  • get their data in a machine-readable format;
  • restrict processing or object to it;
  • withdraw consent at any time.

A pupil aged 13 or older can also withdraw their own consent and ask for their data to be deleted. We tell the parent straight away.

We answer requests as soon as possible and within one month at the latest. For a complex request this can be extended by up to two months; we tell you within the first month if so. You can complain to the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate, Andmekaitse Inspektsioon (Tatari 39, 10134 Tallinn, info@aki.ee, www.aki.ee).

10. Security

The pupil’s data never leaves the phone. The parent view is behind a PIN; we recommend keeping a screen lock on the phone. We keep consent forms and pilot notes locked away, and only the TASA team can access them. If an incident affects data we hold, we report it to the Data Protection Inspectorate without undue delay and where possible within 72 hours, and tell families without delay if it may put them at risk.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, we publish the new version on this page and tell parents about important changes in advance. If TASA ever starts using a server or an e-diary connection, or changes anything else described here, we will ask for new consent first.
